Process

One idea in. Revenue out.

What happens after you sign: kickoff inside 72 hours. We're skipping the part where you pay me; you've seen that movie.

01

The deposition

Ninety minutes, you, zero PR answers. We dig out the stories you forgot were interesting, take apart three rival founders, and lock in what everything runs on: your pillars, your ICP, your dream-guest list, your 500 target accounts.

Your partOne 90-minute chat. Coffee optional, honesty isn't.
02

The production

Scripts first, with hooks that earn the second line. Then one shoot, batched like a heist: founder videos, team skits, B-roll and a voice capture for your AI clone. One shoot day becomes a month of short videos, carousels and a newsletter.

Your partHalf a day on camera, plus one 30-minute approval a week.
03

The show

Your podcast, hosted by you, booked from your target accounts. Four or more guests a month who happen to be people you want to sell to, partner with or raise from. We prep, record, edit, cut around 22 clips, and get you onto other people's shows too.

Your partAbout an hour a week behind the mic. Try not to pitch them.
04

The rollout

Everything ships across you and your team: about five posts a week each on LinkedIn and X. Company pages reach roughly 1.6% of their followers. People reach everyone else.

Your part15 minutes a day in the comments. It's in the playbook.
05

The outbound

500 target accounts a quarter, researched one by one against your ICP. Video-led emails and LinkedIn messages from you, sent from warmed domains, never your main one. Anyone who watched your content jumps the queue.

Your partRecord the week's videos in one batch. Reply to leads within 24 hours.
06

The amplifier

Whatever's already winning gets fuel: 10 new video ads a month cut from your best clips, Thought Leader Ads aimed at your exact job titles, retargeting for everyone who watched, and a clipping network paid per verified view. The rule: beat your cost-per-demo baseline by day 60.

Your partFund the ad budget. On your own card, at cost.
07

The close

Warm meetings with people who already know your team, plus a weekly report on replies, show rate and pipeline. Every month: one call, one page, kill what's flat, double what works. Month two runs on a sharper machine.

Your partShow up to the meetings. Try to look surprised.
