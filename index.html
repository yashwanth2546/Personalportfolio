Process

One idea in. Live launch out.

How a project moves through my hands, from the first conversation to launch day. Seven stops, no surprises.

01

The brief

A conversation, no jargon. I listen for the goal, the audience and what "done" looks like, then tell you honestly which crafts the job needs, and which it does not.

Your partOne honest chat about what you want.
02

The capture

Shoot it, source it or gather it: footage, stills, brand assets, copy. Everything the project will be made from, in one tidy place.

Your partSend what you have. Messy is fine.
03

The sound

Audio first. Voice, music and silence get sorted early, because sound is half of every video, and the half people notice when it is wrong.

Your partPick a mood: a couple of reference tracks is plenty.
04

The cut

The edit and the motion: pacing, titles, kinetic type and transitions. A rough cut first, so we agree on the story before anything gets polished.

Your partWatch the rough cut and tell me what feels off.
05

The build

Where there is a site, it gets built: responsive, fast, and interactive only where that earns its place. You follow along on a live preview link.

Your partOpen the preview, click around, send notes.
06

The lock

Before launch, it gets poked. Inputs checked, dependencies updated, settings tightened. I would rather find the hole than have someone else find it.

Your partNothing. That one is on me.
07

The launch

Everything goes live together: the film, the motion and the site, tidy and in the right formats, with a handover so you are never stuck.

Your partPress the button with me. Then enjoy it.
